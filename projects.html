Security awareness projects

Use these realistic, discussion-led activities to turn security guidance into everyday team habits. Adapt each project for your audience, provide accessible alternatives, and never use real credentials or sensitive personal data in demonstrations.

20-minute workshop

Pause, Check, Report

Teams review sample messages and identify urgency, mismatched sender domains, and unexpected requests. Participants practice reporting a suspicious message through the organization’s approved channel.

Success measure

Anonymous before-and-after confidence check; no message tracking or collection of personal inbox content.

Team exercise

Privacy Permission Checkup

Facilitators guide participants through reviewing app permissions and public profile details on their own devices. Participants can use a fictional example instead of showing personal settings.

Success measure

Count completed learning steps, not individual app choices or personal information.

Case study

The Look-Alike Invoice

A fictional supplier email contains a subtly altered domain and an unexpected payment change. Learners identify verification steps and practice contacting the supplier through a known number.

Debrief prompt

What independent source would you use to verify this request before taking action?
