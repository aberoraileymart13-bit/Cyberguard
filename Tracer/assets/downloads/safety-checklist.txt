CYBERGUARD x TECHFLOW — PERSONAL ONLINE SAFETY CHECKLIST

Take your time. You do not have to do everything at once.

PROTECT YOUR ACCOUNTS
[ ] Use a different, long password for each account.
[ ] Turn on an extra sign-in step when it is available.
[ ] Never share a password or one-time code with a caller or message sender.

CHECK MESSAGES AND WEBSITES
[ ] Pause when a message is unexpected or urgent.
[ ] Read the whole website address before you sign in or pay.
[ ] Use an official app, bookmark, or phone number you already trust.
[ ] Do not pay unexpected fees or buy gift cards to fix a problem.

PROTECT YOUR DEVICE
[ ] Install updates from your device’s settings or official app store.
[ ] Lock your screen when you step away.
[ ] Ask before installing software suggested by an unexpected pop-up or caller.

IF SOMETHING FEELS WRONG
[ ] Stop. Do not reply, click, pay, or share a code.
[ ] Ask someone you trust to help you check.
[ ] Contact the real organization using details you find independently.
[ ] If you shared a password or payment details, tell the account provider promptly.
