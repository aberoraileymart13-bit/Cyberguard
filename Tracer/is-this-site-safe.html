Is this site safe?

Here is who made this learning site, what happens to your information, and how to check other websites.

Who made this site?

CyberGuard x TechFlow made this security-learning site as an educational project.

Your privacy here

  • No account or login is needed.
  • There are no ads and no cookies.
  • You do not need to give personal information to use the lessons. An optional greeting can be left blank; do not enter sensitive details.
  • Your learning progress and display choices stay in this browser on this device.
  • The site does not send your answers, passwords, or saved progress to another service. Password tools work in your browser and do not save what you type.

If you open a hosted copy, your browser still requests the page files from its web host. The site has no tracking or data-submission feature.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses. This site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How to check a website yourself

  1. Pause. Do not sign in or pay from a link in an unexpected message.
  2. Read the full address. Watch for misspellings or extra words in the site name.
  3. Open the organization’s official app, use a bookmark, or type an address you already know.
  4. Contact the organization using a number or address from a trusted source, not from the unexpected message.
  5. A padlock means the connection is protected. It does not prove that the site is genuine.
  6. Ask a trusted person if you are unsure. It is always okay to take more time.
